SIGNATURE EXPERIENCES.

Three pillars that define every visit to ROOFTOP—from first pour to final course.

EXPERIENCE 01

The Dining Experience

Our kitchen celebrates contemporary open-flame grill mastery and delicate Mediterranean-inspired flavours. Whether sharing our Rooftop Signature Platter or savouring Pan-Seared Sea Bass under the stars, every dish is timed to the rhythm of your evening.
