01 / OUR STORY

CONCEIVED AS A SANCTUARY ABOVE THE SKYLINE.

ROOFTOP Restaurant & Lounge Bar was envisioned to elevate metropolitan dining into an immersive nocturnal escape. Perched high above the city streets, the space brings together panoramic horizon sightlines, refined culinary craftsmanship and a warm, social lounge spirit.

From golden hour aperitifs on the open-air terrace to candlelit dinners and late-night live music, every detail is designed so guests feel both connected to the energy of the city below and transported somewhere extraordinary.

02 / OUR DINING PHILOSOPHY

THOUGHTFUL INGREDIENTS. EXPRESSIVE PRESENTATION.

We believe rooftop dining should be both sophisticated and deeply welcoming. Our culinary philosophy centres on balancing bold open-flame grilling with delicate contemporary plating—allowing guests to either share artisanal small plates across a lounge table or savour a multi-course dinner.

Every plate and glass is treated with equal reverence, ensuring that vegetarian creations, prime seafood, signature steaks and zero-proof mocktails share the same standard of excellence.

Seasonal Selection

Freshly sourced produce, premium cuts and aromatic herbs prepared to order.

Harmonious Pairings

Menus designed alongside our mixology team so food and drinks elevate one another.

03 / THE ROOFTOP ATMOSPHERE

DESIGNED FOR EVERY HOUR OF THE EVENING.

Our architectural layout flows seamlessly between open-air horizon terraces, intimate lounge alcoves and an illuminated central cocktail bar.

Open-Air Sky Terrace

Frameless glass balustrades and lush botanical planters frame sweeping views of the sunset and city skyline.

Ambient Lounge Sanctuary

Warm charcoal textures, champagne gold detailing and custom acoustic lighting create an intimate nighttime mood.

Curated Soundscape

From sunset acoustic strings to live jazz ensembles and late-night lounge DJs, music shapes the pulse of the room.

04 / OUR CULINARY EXPERIENCE

CRAFTED IN THE KITCHEN & BEHIND THE BAR.

Our menu brings together signature starters like Truffle Parmesan Fries and Burrata Salad, centrepiece mains such as Prime Grilled Steak and Pan-Seared Sea Bass, and handcrafted cocktails including the Golden Hour Martini and Skyline Spritz.
