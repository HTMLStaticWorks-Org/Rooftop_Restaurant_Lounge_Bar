THE ROOFTOP EXPERIENCE

A HIGHER PERSPECTIVE ON DINING.

Perched high above the metropolitan pulse, ROOFTOP merges architectural open-air terraces, fire-lit lounge alcoves and panoramic horizon dining into three distinct atmospheres.

01 / HORIZON TABLES

Skyline Dining

Intimate candlelit tables along the glass-edge terrace offering unobstructed 270-degree views of the illuminated city skyline.

02 / CRAFT MIXOLOGY

Premium Lounge

Plush velvet seating, a monolithic marble bar counter and avant-garde mixology curated for sophisticated late-night conversations.

03 / GOLDEN HOUR TO DUSK

Sunset Evenings

Watch the sky shift from warm amber to deep midnight indigo while acoustic rhythms and aperitifs set the tone for the night.
